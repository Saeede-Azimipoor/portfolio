Privacy Policy — کتاب مقدس (Persian Bible)

Last updated: October 2026

کتاب مقدس (Persian Bible) does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data.

  • No accounts. The app does not ask you to sign up or sign in.
  • No tracking. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.
  • On-device only. Your favorites, notes, reading position, and settings are stored only on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
  • Fully offline. The app works without an internet connection and makes no network requests.

If you have questions about this policy, contact: info@saeede.dev
